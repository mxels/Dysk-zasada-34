Scan Using TWAIN-Compliant Applications (Macintosh)

  1. Load your document.
  2. Start your graphics application, and then select the scan operation.
    The scanner setup dialog box appears.
  3. Change the scan settings, such as the Resolution, Scan Type, or Adjust Image options, if needed.
  4. Click the Document Size pop-up menu, and then select your document size.
    After you select a document size, adjust the scanning area by holding down the mouse button and dragging your mouse pointer over the portion you want to scan.
  5. Click the PreScan option to preview your image and crop unwanted portions before scanning.
  6. Click Start.
    The machine starts scanning.
