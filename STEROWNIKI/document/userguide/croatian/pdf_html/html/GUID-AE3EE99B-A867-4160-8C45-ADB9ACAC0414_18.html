Change the Resolution and Print Quality (Windows®)

The Resolution and Print Quality options let you select the print resolution. Print quality and speed are related: the higher the quality, the longer it will take to print the document.
  1. Select the print command in your application.
  2. Select Brother MFC-XXXX series (where XXXX is the name of your model), and then click the printing properties or preferences button.
    The printer driver window appears.
  3. Do one of the following:
    • Click the Resolution drop down list, and then select the print resolution you want to use.
      • 300 dpi
      • 600 dpi
      • HQ 1200
    • Click the Print Quality drop down list, and then select the print quality you want to use.
      • Draft
      • Normal
      • Fine
  4. Click OK.
  5. Complete your print operation.
